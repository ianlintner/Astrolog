🚀 Welcome to Space Logistics Tycoon!

Build a galactic trade empire across 100 star systems! You've been given a starter package to begin your journey:

  • 2 Depots in nearby connected systems
  • 2 Transport Ships (1 active, 1 spare)
  • 1 Active Trade Route already earning credits
  • 10,000 Credits for expansion

Quick Start Guide

1. Expand Your Network: Click on star systems to select them, then buy depots (500cr) in systems with good exports/imports.

2. Add Vehicles: Buy more transports (2000cr) to run more routes.

3. Create Routes: Use the dropdowns to connect systems with depots. Match exports → imports for profit!

4. Compete: AI corporations are building routes too. Buy exclusive trade rights (5000cr) to protect your best routes.

Pan: Click & drag the map | Zoom: Mouse wheel | Select: Click systems
